- Rows as in a full extraction: a `set` is one experimental condition -- one block of a results table
  ("Na+ 3.33 mol dm-3, 81 °C"), one entry of a table of independent experiments, or one series of a
  wet-dry experiment; a `obj` is one sample in it -- a time point ("t = 97.3 h"), a cycle ("Cycle 3")
  or a single measurement. A value measured per sample (a yield at a time) goes on its sample; a
  condition of the whole set (salt concentration, temperature) on the set with `obj` ''.
- Compound numbers ("nicotinamide 6", "Yield of 7") and inline citation numbers ("ammonia.14") are
  never values. Name species by name.
