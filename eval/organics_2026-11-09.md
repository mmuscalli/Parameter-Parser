# Organics in target: a drafted data pack vs Prebiotic Chemistry (2026-11-09)

Five papers from `Paper Files/Test Paper List/Organics in target`, each run in developer handoff mode with two data
packs: a new pack drafted from the same five papers (`organics_in_target`, label "Organics in impacts", in the user
packs folder) and the built-in Prebiotic Chemistry pack. Claude Code wrote every answer, so this compares the **packs**
(what each one asks for, how it plans rows, what reaches the CSV) and the **app** around them, not two models.
Suggested variables were accepted where reasonable and remembered, as asked.

| Paper | What it is | Results are in |
|---|---|---|
| mckay1997 (Science) | laser-plasma shocks of cometary gas mixtures | Table 2 (products, molecules per joule) + text |
| takeuchi2020 (Sci. Rep.) | amino acids from shock-recovery impacts on meteorite analogues | Fig. 2 (amounts); conversions in the text |
| muchowska2019 (Nature) | Fe2+-promoted pyruvate + glyoxylate network at 70 °C | Fig. 1b; pH in the Methods text |
| burchell2020 (Sci) | PS / PMMA projectiles at ~5 km/s on aluminium, Raman | text (2 shots, qualitative) |
| mars_delivery2018 (Icarus) | N-body model of carbon delivery to Mars | text (model estimates); tables are inputs |

## The drafted pack

Five Haiku surveys plus one Sonnet design request; $0.069 once (counted with `count_tokens`, Standard prices). The
result had 3 stages: impact conditions and materials (always), results (always), and a delivery-model stage triggered
by keywords. It had 28 parameters and 4 categories. Find compounds then added 55 compounds (51 with PubChem CIDs) and
the chemistry step.

Fixes the draft needed (all now in the app):
- `shock_duration` in µs was turned into `shock_duration_hr`; a 0.7 µs shock would have been stored as 1.9e-10 h.
- PubChem gave the wrong compound for 3 names and none for 3 more: `α-ABA` came back as GABA, which is γ-aminobutyric
  acid; `HKG` became an unrelated drug, although the survey gave "hydroxyketoglutarate"; `α-ketoglutarate`, `β-Ala`
  and `β-AIBA` found nothing. The cause is that PubChem's name search mishandles Greek letters.
- Find compounds removed `product_name` but kept the per-product values that depend on it (amounts in nmol,
  molecules/J). Those rows would no longer say which product they are about.
- The compound categories it added had no review `sources`, so compounds in the review had no heading.
- The chemistry step came without the reaction volume and without the amounts calculation. Muchowska's 0.2 mmol
  pyruvate therefore stayed in mmol instead of becoming 33 mM.
- The delivery-model stage's trigger words ("simulation", "delivery") fired on McKay ("laboratory simulations") and
  Takeuchi ("extraterrestrial delivery"). The cost was about $0.012 each, for no values. I wrote those words as the
  design model, and the design prompt now asks for specific terms.

## Conclusions

- **Use a drafted pack for a new field.** The prebiotic pack's row rules are built for solution chemistry: a text
  result needs a starting composition, a temperature and a duration. They correctly exclude most of these papers' text
  results, so it captured 42% of the reportable values. The drafted pack captured all of them. Its rows are "one
  product (or delivered substance) per condition or population", which fits shock and delivery papers. It also
  carries impact columns (velocity, peak pressure, shock duration) the prebiotic pack only gets through suggestions.
- **Prebiotic is the better pack for aqueous chemistry.** On Muchowska, the one aqueous paper, it was more complete:
  concentrations from amounts and volume, and 20 GC-MS / NMR instrument settings from its analysis step.
- **Fix the drafted pack's results stage.** It duplicates the chemistry step when products are compounds. Recording
  per-product values only in compound columns would save about $0.04 on a McKay-like paper. One option: let the
  chemistry step's printed-unit measure (`sampled_HCN_gas_molecules_per_J`) be the only place, and keep the results
  stage for whole-experiment results.
- **Figure-only and modelling papers.** Two of five papers have results only in figures, and one is a model. Neither
  pack yields rows for figure-only papers without digitizing.

## Changes made in this round

The unit and storage fixes affect every pack:
- **Same unit, different spelling.** "molecules per joule", "molecules J-1" and "molecules/J" are one unit
  (`units.unit_slug`), so stage values in either spelling are kept.
- **New units:** GPa, m/s, km/s, J, kJ, mJ, g/cm3, kg/m3.
- **Columns named after a unit** the app converts (`_GPa`, `_km_per_s`, `_us`, `_nmol`, `_J`, `_g_per_cm3`) read back
  in that unit. Suggested numeric variables get such names, so "~0.9 km/s" is stored as the number 0.9.
- **Approximate values** ("~7 GPa", "about 100 ns") parse; wt% / mol% / vol% read as %.
- **Thousands separators** (1,000 / 12,000 / 4,395,000) are no longer read as decimals, in `units` and `value_types`.
- **Temperature differences** (increase, rise, change, ΔT ...) convert without the K / °C offset.
- **Limits without a limit column.** A value printed as ">x" or "<x" for a parameter with no limit column keeps the
  bound in its provenance and is flagged.
- **Compound values the app can't put in mM** (molecules/J, an amount without a volume, mg/mL) go to the review with
  their printed unit as the measure (`sampled_HCN_gas_molecules_per_J`), instead of being dropped without a trace.
  Starting compositions in % get `_pct` columns (`initial_CH4_gas_pct`), not `_mM`.
- **PubChem:**
  - Greek letters are spelled out before the search.
  - A code whose full name the paper gave, but PubChem didn't find, isn't looked up as a code.

Compound setup for drafted packs:
- The name column is kept while other values still depend on it.
- Compound categories get review sources, and match any measure.
- Switching the chemistry step on adds the amounts calculation and a reaction volume parameter.
- New column suffixes: short times (ms / µs / ns) and amounts (nmol / µmol / mmol).

Workflow and cost:
- **Table-grid rule.** The rule and the `grids` answer field were sent to every table-reading step although table
  grids are off by default. They're now included only when grids are on: prebiotic −1.2K prompt and −3.1K schema
  tokens (about $0.006 a run), exoplanets about $0.015 a run.
- **Figure-only papers in unattended runs** (CLI and queues without hand or automatic digitizing) skip their
  extraction steps, with a flag saying to digitize the figures in the app. A paper with no figures and no rows skips
  them in the app too.
- **Citations taken for captions.** A citation that a line break leaves at the start of a line ("Table 3 for product
  yields.", "Table 1 shows ...") is no longer a table caption. 14 of 148 corpus PDFs now send fewer whole-page
  images, and faisal2005's Table 1 values are attributed to page 3, where the table is (the reference was
  regenerated).
- **Drafting in handoff mode** (developer): surveys and the design request are written for Claude Code to answer.
- **Design prompt:** keyword-triggered stages need specific terms, not general words.
- **Exoplanet pack:** `pl_dens` and `st_dens` declare g/cm3. They were unitless, and "g cm-3" is now a known unit.

## Not fixed

- burchell2020 is an MDPI "corrected version" PDF. Its text layer holds the old and new text in two fonts, offset by
  0.9 pt; the render shows only one. pdfplumber gives no drawing order, so the hidden layer can't be told apart, and
  the text the model reads interleaves the two ("MseeAF Cig₅uHr₉eO1₂"). Table images don't help, because it has no
  tables.
- The table finder sends whole pages for McKay's page 1. Its PDF page holds two journal pages, and Table 1 is under a
  figure, so the crop wasn't found.
- Columns accepted earlier keep their names: remembered `laser_pulse_energy`, `laser_pulse_length`,
  `plasma_temperature`, `stirring_rate`, `projectile_diameter` and `impact_speed` hold text. Rename or retype them in
  Data packs > Remembered from your reviews if you want numbers.
